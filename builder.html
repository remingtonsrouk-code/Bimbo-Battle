Build a new fighter

Every new fighter starts with one style, one trait and two skills, all at Tier I. Work down the steps and the card on the right fills in. When it's done, copy the row and send it to the league owner, who adds it to the Roster tab.

This is an example build so you can see a finished sheet. Change anything, or start from a blank sheet.
1

Name and stable

The league owner checks names against the roster, so use the one you'll write her under.

2

Size band

Size is a band from 1 to 5. It only matters for the Massive trait, which gives +1 on every roll against an opponent two or more bands smaller. Enter her size in cc to find her band, or pick the band directly.

Bands follow a bell curve: most fighters sit in band 3, a quarter each in bands 2 and 4, and only the extremes reach 1 or 5.

3

Style

Her style is the biggest number on her sheet: +1 on every roll in her home match at Tier I, rising to +3 at Tier III. In testing, a style alone won about 73% of fights against an identical fighter without one. The home match is also the one she picks when she sets the match type, and it decides which condition she takes when she loses.

Hybrid styles (Amazon, Harpy, Siren, Nymph) and Champion aren't available at creation. A hybrid needs two base styles first; Champion needs the title.

4

Trait

Pick one trait. It's on for the whole fight. Most traits key off a roll's tag: the match type's tag plus the tag of any skill used that roll. The note under each trait says how often it will fire for the style you picked.

5

Two skills

Pick two Tier I skills. Each is used once per fight and stacks with her style. Skills that share her home tag also wake up tag-based traits, and some skills only work after another one.

6

Preferred match

7

Art (optional)

Four pictures: her roster card, her corner in a fight, her corner at 0 integrity, and a B-KO. Leave any blank and the next one in line is used. Use the file names you'll send the league owner, or full image links.

8

Send it in

The roster row pastes straight into the next empty row of the Roster tab, in the template's column order. The summary is for a message to the league owner.
